The headline of the piece

ARTICLE_MARKER Readability looks for the run of paragraphs that carries the substance of a page, and this is meant to be that run. It needs a fair amount of prose before it will commit to calling something an article, so this paragraph goes on for a while rather than stopping at a sentence.

A second paragraph keeps the character count above the library's own threshold. Without enough text it returns nothing at all, which is the behaviour the fallback exists to cover. Here there is plenty, so an article should come back.

A third paragraph, for good measure, so that the extracted text is comfortably longer than the two hundred characters the extractor insists on before it will prefer an article over the whole page.
